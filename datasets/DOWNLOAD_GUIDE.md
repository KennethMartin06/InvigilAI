# Dataset Download Guide

## Already Included
1. **CMU Keystroke Dynamics** — `keystroke/cmu_keystroke.csv` (4.5 MB, 20,400 sessions)
2. **BALABIT Mouse Dynamics** — `mouse/Mouse-Dynamics-Challenge/` (242 MB)
3. **ESC-50 Metadata** — `audio/ESC-50/meta/esc50.csv` (2,000 environmental sounds)

## Download Manually (registration required or large files)

### Gaze / Eye Tracking
- **MPIIGaze**: https://www.mpi-inf.mpg.de/departments/computer-vision-and-machine-learning/research/gaze-based-human-computer-interaction/appearance-based-gaze-estimation-in-the-wild
- **ETH-XGaze**: https://ait.ethz.ch/xgaze (1M+ images)
- **Columbia Gaze**: https://www.cs.columbia.edu/CAVE/databases/columbia_gaze/

### Student Engagement
- **DAiSEE**: https://iith.ac.in/~daisee-dataset (9,068 video clips)

### Face Detection
- **WIDER FACE**: http://shuoyang1213.me/WIDERFACE/ (already downloaded as zip)

### Pose Estimation
- **COCO Keypoints**: http://images.cocodataset.org/annotations/annotations_trainval2017.zip

### Audio
- **LibriSpeech**: https://us.openslr.org/resources/12/test-clean.tar.gz
- **AudioSet**: https://research.google.com/audioset/download.html
- **MUSAN**: https://us.openslr.org/resources/17/musan.tar.gz

### Video / Action Recognition
- **UCF-101**: https://www.crcv.ucf.edu/data/UCF101/UCF101.rar
- **HMDB-51**: https://serre-lab.clps.brown.edu/resource/hmdb-a-large-human-motion-database/
